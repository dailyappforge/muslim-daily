Privacy Policy

Muslim Daily  |  Last updated: March 2026

Muslim Daily ("we", "our", or "the app") is an Islamic companion application providing prayer times, Qur'an reading, and duas. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information the app accesses, why, and how it is handled.

1. Information We Access or Collect

a) Location

Muslim Daily requests access to your device's location (precise and/or approximate) for the sole purpose of calculating accurate Islamic prayer times for your area. Location data is processed entirely on your device and is never sent to our servers or shared with third parties (other than as described under Advertising below).

If you choose to enter your location manually, GPS is never accessed.

b) Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), IP address, and general usage information to serve ads. This data collection is governed entirely by Google's Privacy Policy . We do not receive or store any advertising data ourselves.

c) App Preferences

Settings you configure (prayer calculation method, notification preferences, Qur'an bookmarks, display preferences) are saved locally on your device using Android's SharedPreferences storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

2. How We Use Your Information

DataPurpose
Location (GPS or manual) Calculate prayer times on-device. Never transmitted externally.
Advertising ID & device info Serve advertisements via Google AdMob (handled entirely by Google).
App preferences & bookmarks Stored locally to personalise your experience. Not accessible to us.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We do not operate any backend servers or user accounts, and we do not collect any data ourselves.

The only third-party service used is Google AdMob for displaying advertisements. Please review Google's Privacy Policy for details on how AdMob handles data.

4. App Permissions Explained

PermissionWhy it is needed
Location (Fine & Coarse) To calculate prayer times based on your geographic position.
Post Notifications To deliver prayer time alerts at the correct times.
Schedule Exact Alarms / Use Exact Alarm To ensure prayer notifications arrive at the precise scheduled time, not delayed by battery optimisation.
Receive Boot Completed To automatically restore prayer notifications after the device is restarted, without requiring you to open the app.
Vibrate To vibrate the device when a prayer notification arrives.
Internet Required to load advertisements from Google AdMob.

5. Data Retention

Because all data is stored locally on your device, it is retained only for as long as the app is installed. Uninstalling Muslim Daily removes all locally stored preferences and data. We retain nothing on any external server.

6. Children's Privacy

Muslim Daily is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app's advertising is not targeted at children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal data to us, please contact us and we will address the matter promptly.

7. Security

Since we do not transmit or store your data on external servers, there is no centralised data store that could be breached. All information remains on your device, protected by your device's own security mechanisms.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Revoke location permission at any time via Android Settings → Apps → Muslim Daily → Permissions.
  • Disable notifications at any time via Android Settings → Apps → Muslim Daily → Notifications, or within the app's Settings screen.
  • Opt out of personalised ads by resetting or opting out of the Advertising ID in your device's Google Settings.
  • Delete all locally stored app data by clearing app data via Android Settings → Apps → Muslim Daily → Storage → Clear Data, or by uninstalling the app.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
